# Privacy Policy
**Last Updated: [09/08/2026]**

This Privacy Policy explains how the DG AI Discord Bot (the "Bot") and the DG Develops ecosystem collect, use, and protect your information when you interact with our services.

## 1. Information We Collect
We collect minimal data necessary to operate the Bot and ensure platform security:

* **Discord User ID**: Collected when you use the /verify command to link your Turnstile verification to your Discord account and assign roles.
* **Message Content**: Collected temporarily when you use the /summary command. This message content is sent to the DG AI API to generate a summary.
* **Discord Guild/Server IDs & Channel IDs**: Used to register slash commands and route announcements via the /announce command.
* **Cloudflare Turnstile Token**: A cryptographic token generated by Cloudflare to verify you are human. This token is sent to our Cloudflare Worker for validation.

## 2. How We Use Your Information
* **To Provide Service**s: Processing your /ask and /summary requests via the DG AI API.
* **To Maintain Security**: Using Cloudflare Turnstile to prevent automated bot attacks and assign verified roles to legitimate users.
* **To Operate the Bot**: Reading channel messages to summarize them and sending messages to designated channels.
  
## 3. Data Storage and Retention
* **Verification Data**: When you use /verify, your User ID and verification status are stored temporarily in Cloudflare KV (Key-Value Store). This data is strictly ephemeral. It is automatically deleted ("vanishes") immediately after the Discord Bot confirms your status and assigns your role or kicks you. We do not keep a permanent record of verification attempts.
* **AI Prompts**: Messages sent to the DG AI API are processed at the edge via Cloudflare Workers to generate a response. We do not permanently store your conversations or message summaries.
* **Logs**: The Cloudflare Worker and the Discord Bot may generate temporary error logs for debugging purposes. These logs do not contain sensitive personal information and are rotated regularly.

## 4. Third-Party Services
Your data is processed by the following third-party services to deliver our functionalities:

* **Discord**: For bot communication, role assignment, and message reading.
* **Cloudflare**: For Turnstile human verification, Cloudflare Workers (backend processing), and Cloudflare KV (temporary storage).
* **Render**: For hosting the Discord Bot application.
* **Netlify**: For hosting the verification frontend webpage.

*(Note: These third parties have their own privacy policies governing how they handle data).*

## 5. Data Sharing
We do not sell, trade, or rent your personal information to third parties. We only share data as necessary to operate the service (e.g., sending a message to the DG AI API for a response).

## 6. Your Rights
As we do not maintain a permanent database of your personal data, there is no stored user data for you to request deletion of. If you wish to "delete" your presence, simply remove the Bot from your server or stop using its commands.

## 7. Changes to This Privacy Policy
We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time to reflect changes in our practices or legal requirements. We will notify users of significant changes by updating the "Last Updated" date at the top of this policy.

## 8. Contact
If you have questions or concerns about this Privacy Policy or how your data is handled, please contact us via the official DG Develops Discord server.
